Interactive forecast · runs in your browser

Predict any hour — 1 to 72 hours ahead.

Pick a starting hour and a horizon; a TensorFlow.js copy of the trained model recursively forecasts forward right here in your browser — no server, nothing sent anywhere — feeding each prediction back in as its own lag/rolling features, exactly like a real deployment would. Since there's no live weather feed, the forecast window is limited to the historical period, using recorded weather as a stand-in for a forecast feed. That also means you get a ground-truth line to compare against, wherever it's available.
